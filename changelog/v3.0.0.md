# v3.0.0

A ground-up rewrite. Everything the v2 app did, it still does — reorganized along Symfony
conventions, with the two abandoned v2 work streams finished and the model changes kept to the
smallest additive step that delivers them.

## Breaking: shop schema v3

Shops carry a major version, so v1 and v2 shops must be migrated before they open. The built-in
migration wizard does this for you — see below. Two columns are added, both optional and
appended at the end of their sheets:

- `jobs.due_date` — a real due date, instead of inferring it from the creation date
- `inventory.colour` — a swatch colour for filament spools

## Audit log — finished

Every change to your shop is now recorded: who did it, when, what changed, and what triggered it.

- All create / update / archive / delete / restore operations write an audit entry automatically,
  including cascaded changes, which record the parent that caused them
- Entries capture the full before and after state plus the exact list of changed fields
- The Audit Log page resolves entries to human-readable names with links to the entity, and adds
  search plus action and entity-type filters

## Migration wizard — now actually migrates

The v2 wizard explained the migration but its Continue button did nothing. It works now.

- Detects the version gap and chains the migration (a v1 shop runs v1→v2→v3 in one pass)
- Asks whether to keep a backup, and shows live per-entity progress
- Runs against an isolated copy of your shop; the version flip is the final step, so a failure
  leaves your original shop untouched and re-openable
- Backfills audit entries for pre-existing rows, adds the archive/delete columns, and adds the
  new v3 columns. No data is removed.

## New

- **Calendar view** on the dashboard — see jobs on their due dates, month grid on desktop and a
  day list on mobile
- **Kanban cards show piece progress**, alongside the pricing and benefit they already showed
- **Inventory colours** with swatches in the inventory list

## Changed

- Returning to the app no longer sends you back to the setup wizard: your shop is remembered
  across tabs and restarts. The Google access token is never stored — it is held in memory and
  renewed silently, which is both safer and invisible in use.
- Due dates are now editable on jobs, and prefill from the shop's `defaultDueDate` setting

## Under the hood

- Entities are real classes owning their own mapping and invariants; repositories sit behind
  interfaces with Local CSV and Google Sheets implementations; domain services take their
  dependencies by constructor injection
- Migrations are declarative plans of idempotent steps, so future model changes are additive work
- 100% test coverage is enforced by the test suite's thresholds
