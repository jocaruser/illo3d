Documentation only; no application behaviour changes.

- The not-found spec links the archive-then-delete rule that keeps deleted records out of the app
- Decision records are numbered 0001 to 0017 with no gap: fuzzy matching is ADR-0016 and Google Drive end-to-end mocking is ADR-0017
