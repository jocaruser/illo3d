# A client's page

A page at `#/clients/{id}`,
with [everything a details page has](../../shared/details.spec.md),
top to bottom:

- **Who they are** — the recorded fields, shown only when filled:
  id, email, phone, preferred contact,
  lead source (mentions in it become links —
  [notes](../../shared/notes.spec.md#mentions)),
  address, created date,
  and the free-text note from the client record.
- [The widgets](widgets.spec.md) — their money story in five figures.
- [Tags](../../shared/tags.spec.md) and [notes](../../shared/notes.spec.md).
- [The activity timeline](../../shared/timeline.spec.md).
- [Their jobs](jobs-table.spec.md).
