# Upgrade: v2 → v3

What a version-2 shop gains when the
[migration wizard](wizard.spec.md) brings it to version 3.

## Due dates on jobs

Jobs gain an optional due date.
Until one is set, a job's age is measured from its creation date,
exactly as before —
so upgraded shops look unchanged until due dates are used.
Due dates power the dashboard's calendar view
and the colour-coded lateness badges.

## Colours on materials

Materials gain an optional colour
(think filament spools),
shown as a small swatch beside the material's name
in the inventory.

## What the user sees during the run

Two cards:
**Jobs** — "Adding the due date column to jobs" —
and **Inventory** — "Adding the colour column to inventory".

Nothing is removed or altered —
existing data is only extended.
